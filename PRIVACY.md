# Oculo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Oculo is a mobile spatial-cinematography app developed by Kore Computing Labs. This page explains what the app does with your data. Oculo is local-first: your work stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

## What stays on your device
- **Projects.** Project names, notes, saved shots, camera settings and moves are stored in the app's on-device storage.
- **Scene files you import.** A `.spz` file you choose is copied into the app's on-device storage. Oculo does not upload it, and it does not put the file's original path or contents into project metadata.
- **Exports.** PNG shot sheets and video previews are created on your device. They leave the app only when you use the share sheet or a download and choose a destination.

Deleting a project removes it from the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything Oculo stored. Copies you already exported or shared are not deleted.

## Camera
The Magic Window feature asks for camera access so your iPhone can track its motion and let you move the phone like a handheld camera inside a scene. Oculo receives motion and tracking information only. It does not record, store or upload photos or video of your surroundings.

## Network use
- **Online demo scenes.** If you open a scene that isn't bundled with the app, it is downloaded from a public web host. That host sees the usual connection information, such as your IP address.
- **Links.** Opening a credit, license, privacy, terms or support link opens it in your browser.
- Oculo does not include analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Purchases
Oculo Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the one-saved-project limit. Purchases are handled by your app store (Apple or Google) and by RevenueCat, the service Oculo uses to manage the purchase and unlock. RevenueCat assigns an anonymous customer identifier and processes purchase and entitlement information to do this. Oculo never sees or stores your payment card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and your app store's terms for how they handle that data.

## Accounts and cloud backup
Accounts and cloud backup are optional features and are turned off unless the person building the app configures them. When they are off, Oculo makes no account or backup network requests.

## Children
Oculo does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
